Make a donation

Every donation goes toward lowering what families pay to play. We keep our fees well below the big clubs so every kid has the opportunity to play competitive baseball, no matter what their family can afford.

Sharks Nation Incorporated is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit as classified by the IRS, so your donation may be tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law. For gifts over $1,000, please email Info@SharksNationBaseball.com.
