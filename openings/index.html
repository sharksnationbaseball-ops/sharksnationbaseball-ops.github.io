Age chart

Age groups use a May 1 cutoff: your player’s age group is their age on April 30 of that season. For example, a player who is 8 years old on April 30, 2027 plays 8U in 2027. Ages 4 and 5 play T-Ball, and ages 6 and 7 play machine pitch.
