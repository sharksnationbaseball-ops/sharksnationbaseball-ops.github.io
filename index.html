About Sharks Nation

Sharks Nation started in Florissant, MO in 2015 with two teams and has grown into a full program from T-Ball through 15U. Our players train with former and current major league, minor league, and college players, along with some of the best high school coaches in the area.

We develop players from the recreational level to the elite level by teaching proper fundamentals, and we prepare them to compete at higher levels of the game. Tournament teams play a full summer schedule of local and out-of-town events, a fall season, and a winter training program.

Our mission

We are committed to empowering our student-athletes to reach their full potential in academics, community engagement, and athletics, sustained through accountability, consistency, leadership, and pride.

How to join

Roster spots open throughout the year. Here's how a player becomes a Shark.

  1. Reach out

    Call or email us with your player's age group. We'll tell you which teams have openings.

  2. Try out

    Come to a practice or tryout and show the coaching staff what you can do.

  3. Sign on

    Accept your roster offer, sign the season contract, and get fitted for your uniform.
